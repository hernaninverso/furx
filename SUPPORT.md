# Support

Thanks for using Furx! Here's where to go, depending on what you need.

## Questions, ideas, show & tell

Use **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/hernaninverso/furx/discussions)** for usage questions,
ideas, and sharing what you built. It's the best place for anything open-ended — please search first to
avoid duplicates.

## Bug reports & feature requests

Open a **[GitHub Issue](https://github.com/hernaninverso/furx/issues/new/choose)** using the templates:

- **Bug report** — include your Furx version, OS, install method, and clear repro steps. Scrub any keys,
  tokens, hostnames, or personal data from logs before pasting.
- **Feature request** — describe the problem you're solving, not just the solution.

Issues are for confirmed bugs and concrete proposals; for open questions, prefer Discussions.

## Security vulnerabilities

**Do not** open a public issue. Follow the private disclosure process in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) (email `security@furx.cloud` or a GitHub Security Advisory).

## Commercial support

Pro, Team, and Enterprise customers get priority support — see
[furx.cloud/pricing](https://furx.cloud/pricing). General product questions: `support@furx.cloud`.

## Before you ask

- Check the [README](README.md), the [`docs/`](docs/) folder, and existing issues/discussions.
- Include enough detail to reproduce — version, OS, exact steps, and what you expected vs. saw.
