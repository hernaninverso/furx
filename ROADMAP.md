# Roadmap

Direction, not dated promises. Priorities shift with feedback — open a
[Discussion](https://github.com/hernaninverso/furx/discussions) or an issue to weigh in.
Statuses: ✅ shipped · 🚧 in progress · 🔲 planned.

## Now (next ~3 months)

- 🚧 **Mobile companion (iOS, read-only)** — pair over the local bridge to view panes, approve
  tool-calls, and replay the audit log. Currently scaffolding.
- 🚧 **Web companion** — read-only audit replay in the browser.
- 🔲 **Cost-Router** — auto-divert low-stakes calls to cheaper/free models with a savings meter.
- 🔲 **Onboarding polish** — health-check the connected providers from the Connect wizard.

## Next (3–9 months)

- 🔲 **Plugin marketplace** — discover and install signed MCP plugins from the registry.
- 🔲 **Knowledge-graph UI** — browse and query the Memory Hub graph visually.
- 🔲 **Team admin console** — seats, shared cost dashboard, centralized audit.
- 🔲 **Linux/Windows polish** — parity passes on packaging, tray, and updater.

## Later

- 🔲 **Collaborative sessions** — shared panes with presence.
- 🔲 **Deeper agent orchestration** — pipelines/DAGs across panes.
- 🔲 **Self-host admin** — turnkey Enterprise deployment.

## Out of scope (by design)

- Hosting or proxying your model traffic — Furx is BYOK and local-first on principle.
- Storing your provider keys anywhere but the OS keychain.

Have an idea or a use case we're missing? [Start a discussion.](https://github.com/hernaninverso/furx/discussions)
