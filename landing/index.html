Connect whatever you already have

Pure BYOK across 15 providers: your keys live in your OS keychain and every call goes straight from your machine to the provider — Furx is never in the request path. The Furx Connect wizard offers five paths; mix and match freely.

OpenRouter Quick Start

One key, hundreds of models. Council Mode picks distinct model families from the catalog — Claude, GPT, Gemini, Qwen, DeepSeek, Llama — and fills all six voices. About 30 seconds of onboarding.

Free tiers

Separate free accounts, one key each: Cerebras · Groq · Mistral · SambaNova · Google Gemini AI Studio. Daily quotas vary per provider; Furx orchestrates whatever you connect. $0/mo.

Paid APIs, direct

Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini API — your own keys, your billing, your rate limits. Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works too.

Local inference

Auto-detects Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, vLLM. With a local-only council, model inference never leaves your machine. $0 ongoing.

Gateways / LiteLLM

Paste your org gateway URL. Centralized governance and spend tracking. Anything that speaks OpenAI-compatible JSON works.

Mix-and-match

Combine all five paths. The council dispatches across whichever providers are healthy, with graceful degradation if one voice rate-limits.

What each tier adds

The free core ships everything that runs on your machine — all panes, all hotkeys, Council Mode, the audit log, Memory Hub, BYOK. Paid tiers add hosted services. No feature is ever removed from the free tier after being added.

Local inference — privacy by default

Furx detects Ollama on localhost:11434 and lists the loaded models. Connect it in one click. With the local council preset, model inference never leaves your machine — useful for legal, regtech, and finance teams, or for anyone who doesn't want tokens leaving their laptop. 

$ ollama pull qwen2.5-coder:32b
$ ollama pull deepseek-r1:14b
$ ollama pull llama3.3:70b
# then in Furx → Settings → Connect → Local tab → 3 models ✓
# Council Mode preset "local" = 3 parallel local voices
